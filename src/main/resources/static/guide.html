1. What is a Bachat Gat? The Domain Fundamentals

Understanding the socio-economic foundation and operational cycle of Self-Help Groups in India

A Bachat Gat (Self-Help Group or SHG / महिला बचत गट) is a community-based microfinance group typically comprising 10 to 20 women from similar socio-economic backgrounds. Guided by India's National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM) and NABARD guidelines, the group pools small regular savings, deposits them in a collective bank account, and provides low-interest internal loans to members for agriculture, small enterprises, healthcare, or emergencies.

Core Philosophy: Mutual trust, collective financial empowerment, and zero reliance on high-cost informal moneylenders. All interest earned on member loans is not an external profit—it belongs to the group, expanding the total corpus and distributed back to members as annual dividends.

The Real-World Monthly Cycle

Step 1 Monthly Savings Pool

Every member commits to saving a fixed amount (e.g. ₹5,000) by a designated due date (e.g. 10th of every month). 20 members x ₹5,000 = ₹1,00,000 monthly fresh capital.

Step 2 Internal Lending

Members apply for loans. Group leaders (President, Secretary, Treasurer) review the request during the monthly meeting, set repayment tenure and interest rate, and disburse funds.

Step 3 Repayment & Compounding

Borrowers repay EMIs (Principal + Interest). The principal returns to the savings pool for new loans, while interest increases group earnings and member net worth.

Why Digital Software is Essential

Traditionally, Bachat Gats maintain handwritten notebooks (हस्ताक्षरातील वह्या). This suffers from severe drawbacks:

  • Human Calculation Mistakes: Calculating reducing-balance compound interest and apportioning principal vs interest is error-prone.
  • Disputes Over Collections: Missing receipts lead to arguments regarding who paid, who defaulted, or who owes late penalties.
  • No Real-Time Visibility for Members: Members only learn their cumulative savings or loan balance by asking the treasurer once a month.
  • Audit & Bank Linkage Hurdles: Commercial banks demand transparent, auditable digital records before sanctioning SHG credit linkage loans.

This platform digitizes every single step—providing an automated, transparent, multi-tenant cloud banking solution tailored for Bachat Gats.

3. User Roles, Security & Multi-Tenancy

Strict tenant boundary isolation and granular role-based authorization

Security and financial confidentiality are paramount. A member of Mahila Pragati Bachat Gat must never be able to view financial transactions, savings, or member records of Saraswati Bachat Gat.

ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN System Administrator

High-level administrative authority. Can create new Bachat Gats, manage client admins, monitor system health, inspect global audit trails, and review cross-group analytics.

Portal: /admin/super-dashboard.html
ROLE_ADMIN Group President / Secretary

Group Tenant Admin: Scoped strictly to their assigned groupId (e.g., bg-001). Manages members, configures interest rate policies, sanctions loans, logs savings collections, expenses, and meeting minutes.

Portal: /admin/dashboard.html
ROLE_USER Individual SHG Member

Strict Single-Member Privacy: Scoped strictly to their own member ID. Can view personal savings balance, passbook transactions, loan repayment schedule, and apply for loans online.

Portal: /user/dashboard.html

Multi-Tenant Isolation Enforcement

Multi-tenancy is enforced at three distinct checkpoints:

  1. JWT Claims Injection: During authentication, the backend signs the user's assigned groupId and memberId directly into the cryptographic JWT token payload.
  2. Spring Security Context: Controller requests extract the logged-in user's identity via @AuthenticationPrincipal UserDetails or SecurityContextHolder.
  3. Database Scoped Queries: Every repository query filters records by WHERE group.id = :groupId. Group admins attempting to query another group's ID receive an immediate 403 Access Denied error.

5. Module-by-Module Deep Dive

Detailed breakdown of every subsystem, database entity, REST API, and business rules

Module 1: Authentication, User Management & First-Login Security

Security Subsystem

Handles user authentication, password salting with BCrypt, JSON Web Token (JWT) generation, first-time login mandatory password change, and automatic routing to role-specific dashboards.

Key Files & Artifacts

  • Backend: com.bachatgat.controller.AuthController.java
  • Backend: com.bachatgat.security.JwtAuthenticationFilter.java, JwtTokenProvider.java
  • Backend: com.bachatgat.model.User.java, Role.java
  • Frontend: /login.html, /first-login.html, /js/auth.js, /js/api.js

Authentication Workflow

  1. User enters username & password at /login.html.
  2. Request sent to POST /api/auth/login.
  3. DaoAuthenticationProvider verifies BCrypt hashed password in User table.
  4. If user.isFirstLogin() == true, user is redirected to /first-login.html to force a secure password change before accessing any financial records.
  5. Successful login issues a JWT bearer token. Client stores token in localStorage and routes:
    • ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN → /admin/super-dashboard.html
    • ROLE_ADMIN → /admin/dashboard.html
    • ROLE_USER → /user/dashboard.html

Module 3: Master Data & Dynamic Interest Rate Policy

Configurable Financial Rules
Manual Interest Rate Customization: In response to Bachat Gat operational requirements, interest rates are never fixed or hardcoded. Each group's admin has full manual control to customize the interest rate in both Monthly (%/month) and Annual (% p.a.) terms, along with the calculation method.

Configurable Policy Parameters

  • Monthly Bachat (₹): The fixed monthly savings contribution per member (e.g. ₹2,000, ₹5,000).
  • Collection Due Day: Calendar day each month when dues must be paid (e.g. 10th).
  • Loan Interest Rate (% / month & % p.a.): Two-way synchronized inputs. E.g., setting 2% per month automatically sets 24% p.a.
  • Loan Calculation Type:
    • REDUCING_BALANCE: EMI decreases interest burden as principal is repaid.
    • FLAT: Interest is charged on initial principal across entire duration.
  • Maximum Loan Limit (₹): Upper ceiling on member borrowability (e.g. ₹1,00,000).
  • Late Payment Penalty (₹): Flat fee per overdue occurrence (e.g. ₹100.00).
  • Dividend Allocation: % of annual profit distributed back to members (e.g. 100%).

Where to Configure

Group admins can configure these rules via /admin/master-data.html (Master Data Settings) or directly in /admin/groups.html under the "Financial Rules & Policies" modal.

Module 4: Member Registration, KYC & Directory

Membership Subsystem

Manages digital onboarding of members. Every member is assigned a unique member code (e.g. MEM-001) and linked to a system user account for self-service portal access.

Member Data Model Highlights

  • Personal Info: Full name, Marathi/regional name, date of birth, mobile number, residential address.
  • KYC Identifiers: Masked Aadhaar number (XXXX-XXXX-1234) and PAN number with verification status badges.
  • Bank Account: Member's personal savings account for electronic loan disbursements.
  • Nominee Details: Nominee name, relationship, and contact for emergency settlement.
  • Financial Standing: Tracks totalSavingsAccumulated, active loans, and status (ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, EXITED).

Module 5: Monthly Savings Collections & Due Generation Engine

Automated Billing Engine

The recurring engine that drives group capitalization. Every month, the platform generates collection records for all active members.

Idempotent Due Generation: The generation algorithm uses a unique composite constraint (groupId, memberId, collectionMonth, collectionYear). Clicking "Generate Monthly Due" multiple times will never duplicate dues or double-charge members.

Collection Status State Transitions

Payment Recording Features

  • Payment Modes: CASH (physical meeting collection), UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), BANK_TRANSFER (NEFT / IMPS).

  • Digital Receipt: Instant receipt number (e.g. REC-202610-0012) printable or downloadable as PDF.

Micro-Lending Engine

Provides complete lifecycle automation from loan application by the member, admin committee review, manual interest rate configuration, sanctioning, disbursement, monthly EMI schedule generation, to full loan closure.

Manual Interest Rate Customization During Sanction

When an admin opens the sanction modal in /admin/loans.html or /admin/loan-applications.html, the platform provides:

  • Monthly Interest Rate (% / month): E.g., 1.50% or 2.00%.
  • Annual Interest Rate (% p.a.): Automatically calculates Monthly x 12 (e.g. 18.00% or 24.00%).
  • Live EMI Preview: Dynamically calculates monthly EMI, total interest, and total payable in real time before sanctioning.
  • Disbursement Mode: Bank transfer reference number or cash voucher note.

Accounting Integrity

Every financial event in the platform logs an immutable Transaction record. This ensures strict compliance with double-entry accounting principles and eliminates discrepancy between cash-in-hand, bank balances, and member ledgers.

6. Financial Math & Loan Calculation Formulas

Precise mathematical formulation for Reducing Balance EMI vs Flat Interest Rate

Bachat Gats in India use two different methodologies for loan interest calculations. The platform supports both dynamically.

Method 1 Reducing Balance (घटती बाकी)

Interest is charged only on the remaining unpaid principal each month. As the borrower repays principal, the monthly interest shrinks.

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1] Where: P = Principal Loan Amount (e.g. ₹50,000) r = Monthly Interest Rate (e.g. 2% = 0.02) n = Tenure in Months (e.g. 12)
Example (₹50,000 @ 2%/mo for 12 mos):
Monthly EMI: ₹4,727.98
Total Interest Paid: ₹6,735.75
Total Repayment: ₹56,735.75
Method 2 Flat Interest (सरळ व्याज)

Interest is calculated on the entire initial principal for the full duration, regardless of repayments made.

Total Interest = P × (Annual Rate / 100) × (n / 12) EMI = (Principal + Total Interest) / n Where: P = Principal Loan Amount (e.g. ₹50,000) Annual Rate = Monthly Rate × 12 (e.g. 2% × 12 = 24%) n = Tenure in Months (e.g. 12)
Example (₹50,000 @ 2%/mo for 12 mos):
Total Interest: ₹50,000 × 24% × 1 = ₹12,000.00
Monthly EMI: (₹50,000 + ₹12,000) / 12 = ₹5,166.67
Total Repayment: ₹62,000.00
Indian SHG Interest Rate Conventions: In rural Maharashtra and across India, SHG women traditionally state rates as "२ रुपये शेकडा" (2 Rupees per hundred per month = 2% per month). The platform automatically converts and synchronizes Monthly % x 12 = Annual % so both parties have complete clarity.

9. Step-by-Step Operator Runbooks

Practical execution workflows for daily Bachat Gat administrators

Runbook 1: How to Change Group Loan Interest Rate

  1. Log in as Group Admin → Navigate to Group Profile (/admin/groups.html).
  2. Click Edit Group Details button.
  3. Under the Financial Rules & Policies section, modify the Monthly Interest Rate (% / month). Notice how the Annual Interest Rate (% p.a.) syncs automatically!
  4. Select the preferred Interest Calculation Method (Reducing Balance or Flat Rate).
  5. Click Save Changes. The new rate immediately governs all subsequent loan applications and member rule views.

Runbook 2: Running the Monthly Collection Meeting

  1. On meeting day, navigate to Collections (/admin/collections.html).
  2. Click Generate Monthly Due. The system populates dues for all active members.
  3. As each member submits their cash or UPI payment, click Record Payment.
  4. Select payment mode (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer), enter reference number, and click Confirm Payment.
  5. Click Print Receipt to hand over the physical receipt slip or SMS notification.

Runbook 3: Processing and Disbursing a Member Loan

  1. Open Loan Applications (/admin/loan-applications.html) to review submitted requests.
  2. Inspect the member's total accumulated savings and past repayment discipline.
  3. Click Sanction / Approve to open the interactive Sanction Modal.
  4. Verify or manually adjust the Sanction Amount, Tenure (Months), and Interest Rate.
  5. Inspect the dynamic EMI preview and click Approve & Disburse. Funds are disbursed, transaction is logged, and the 12-month schedule is live.

10. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Solutions to common operational questions and edge cases

Q: Can different loans within the same Bachat Gat have different interest rates?

Yes! The master data rate serves as the default group baseline, but the Group Admin can manually override the interest rate for individual loans during the sanctioning process (e.g. charging 1.5% for an emergency medical loan vs 2% for a general loan).

Q: What happens if a member pays only part of their monthly savings share?

The collection status transitions to PARTIAL. The system accurately records the amount paid, maintains the remaining balance as outstanding due, and credits the partial amount to the member's cumulative savings passbook.

Q: Can a member view other members' loan amounts or balances?

Never. The platform enforces strict single-member privacy. When logged in under ROLE_USER, APIs restrict all queries strictly to the authenticated member's ID.
